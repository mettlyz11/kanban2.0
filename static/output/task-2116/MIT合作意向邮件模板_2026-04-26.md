# 合作意向邮件模板（基础版，可个性化）

## 中文思路
- 开场：说明关注到对方 2026 年在顶刊发表的 AI 材料相关工作
- 建联理由：北航在 AI 催化与材料智能研究方面有扎实积累
- 合作建议：围绕 AI 催化剂设计、数据驱动材料发现、实验自动化闭环开展交流
- 邀请动作：线上交流 / 未来互访 / 学生联合培养

## 英文邮件模板
Subject: Potential academic collaboration on AI-driven materials research

Dear Prof. [Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I recently read your 2026 paper published in [Journal], entitled "[Paper Title]", and was impressed by your group's work on [Specific Direction]. The way your team combines [Method/Insight] with materials research is particularly relevant to several directions we are actively developing at Beihang University.

I am Liu Yuzhou, Professor at the School of Chemistry, Beihang University. Our recent work focuses on AI-enabled catalyst discovery, data-driven materials research, and the integration of computational modeling with automated/closed-loop experimentation. We believe there may be meaningful overlap between your group's expertise in [Their Direction] and our efforts in [Our Direction].

Possible areas for discussion include:
1. AI-guided catalyst and functional material discovery;
2. Machine-learning models for structure–property–performance relationships;
3. Integration of theory, data, and automated experimentation for accelerated materials development;
4. Student exchange, joint seminars, or exploratory collaborative projects.

If you find this interesting, I would be very glad to arrange a brief online meeting at your convenience. We would also be happy to share a short overview of our representative work and discuss whether there is a suitable starting point for collaboration.

Best regards,
Liu Yuzhou
Professor, School of Chemistry, Beihang University
Beijing, China
