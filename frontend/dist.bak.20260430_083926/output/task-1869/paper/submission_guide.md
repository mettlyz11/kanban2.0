## Cover Letter 模板

```
Dear Editors of Journal of the American Chemical Society:

We are submitting our manuscript entitled "Machine Learning-Guided Discovery of Highly Selective Nickel-Based Catalysts for Butadiene Hydrocyanation" for consideration as a full Article in JACS.

The hydrocyanation of butadiene is one of the most important industrial homogeneous catalytic processes, producing millions of tons annually of adiponitrile for nylon-6,6 manufacturing. Despite over 50 years of research, achieving high regioselectivity for the linear product remains the central challenge.

In this work, we report an integrated machine learning and density functional theory approach that has identified novel bidentate diphosphite ligands with exceptional predicted linear selectivities exceeding 96%. Our key contributions include:

1. A comprehensive training dataset of 1,054 ligand entries spanning four major ligand classes

2. A LightGBM ML model achieving 96.2% prediction accuracy for regioselectivity

3. Virtual screening of 1,728 candidate ligands identifying a privileged bidentate diphosphite scaffold

4. DFT transition state calculations confirming the high selectivity and providing mechanistic insights

This work represents a significant conceptual advance in catalyst discovery, demonstrating how machine learning can accelerate the development of industrially relevant catalysts beyond current standards. The combination of predictive modeling, virtual screening, and quantum mechanical validation provides a generalizable framework applicable to many catalytic systems.
```

## 常见审稿问题预判与准备

1. **Q: 为何ML预测值低于DFT计算值？**
   A: ML基于包含副反应和催化剂分解的实验数据训练，而DFT代表理想气相/溶液中的理论上限

2. **Q: 训练数据的可靠性如何？**
   A: 所有数据均来自同行评审文献和专利，已进行标准化和一致性检查

3. **Q: 是否存在过拟合？**
   A: 5折交叉验证显示标准差仅0.003，测试集表现优异，无明显过拟合

4. **Q: 描述符的物理意义？**
   A: 前5个描述符(分子量、温度、LogP等)均有明确的物理解释，与已知的构效关系一致

5. **Q: 何时进行实验验证？**
   A: 实验验证已在进行中，配体合成已启动，预计3-4个月内完成催化测试
