Dear Prof. Buehler,

I hope this message finds you well. I am Yuzhou Liu, Professor at the School of Chemistry, Beihang University, Beijing. My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and catalytic materials science.

Your quote "An AI that only predicts is an oracle. An AI Scientist closes the loop" perfectly captures our own philosophy — we have been building closed-loop AI-experiment systems for catalyst optimization at Beihang.

Would you be open to a conversation about potential synergies?

With great admiration for your work,
